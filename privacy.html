PerfectPass Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

PerfectPass ("the app", "we") is a biometric ID and passport photo maker for Android and iOS. This policy describes exactly what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Your photo never leaves your device. Capture, face detection, background removal, compliance checking, and print-sheet layout all run on-device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server we control — we don't have a server.
  • We don't have accounts, logins, or a backend. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing tied to your identity at all.
  • There is no advertising. Neither app contains an advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, or any code that makes a network request.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play / the App Store. We never see your payment details — only whether Google/Apple confirms you bought it.

What we collect

Your photos and saved documents — not collected. Every photo you capture or import, every edit, every compliance check, and every saved document stays in the app's private storage on your device. Saved documents are excluded from cloud device backups by design. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently; you can also delete them yourself at any time from Privacy & unlock → Delete all saved documents.

Camera. The app requests camera access to let you take a photo. The camera feed is processed live, on-device, to draw the framing guide overlay; nothing from the camera is transmitted anywhere.

Advertising identifier — not collected, on either platform. Earlier versions of the Android app carried a single banner ad and, with your consent, used your device's advertising identifier for it. That has been removed: there is no advertising SDK in either app, no consent prompt, and no advertising identifier is requested or read.

Purchase status. Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit tell the app what you've bought (a €0.99 single photo, or the €5.99 lifetime unlock, on either platform). We don't receive or store your card details, billing address, or any other payment information — that's handled entirely by Google/Apple.

What we don't collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar, messages, health data, or any account credentials — because the app has no account system and no backend to send any of this to.

Children

PerfectPass is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Because the app has no account system, we have no way to knowingly identify a child user in the first place.

Your choices

  • Delete everything on-device: Privacy & unlock → Delete all saved documents. Immediate and permanent.
  • Delete all app data: uninstall the app. Since nothing is stored on a server, this is complete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. We won't reduce your rights under this policy without your consent for data already collected.
