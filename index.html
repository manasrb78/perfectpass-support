How it works

Snap, format, print. The rules are already known.

Snap

Pick your country and document. A live checklist shows every requirement as you meet it, and auto-capture counts down and shoots when they all pass.

Format

The crop lands the eye line and head height where your document asks. The background is replaced on your phone, and zoom, pan and straighten are there if you want to nudge it.

Print

Tile copies on a 10×15, 11×13 or 13×15 cm sheet with cut guides. Print at home, or save the sheet and take it to a print kiosk.
